# Cyborg Domestic — kit for Grok Imagine

A 43-second vertical comic-book short with original characters: ROOK, a cyborg (chrome face plate, red optic and chrome
arm on his LEFT side), DANA, and BOLT, a cyborg baby. A break-in turns into a romance and ends in domestic comedy.

- `keyframes/s01.jpg` ... `s17.jpg` — the 17 drawn frames, 1080x1920, in story order.
- `PROMPTS.md` — one motion prompt per shot, with its length; hero shots listed first.

For each shot: image-to-video from its keyframe with its prompt, 9:16, the shortest length, sound on; keep the comic
look and never add anything that is not in the keyframe. Name each clip `<shot>.mp4`.
